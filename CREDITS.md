Every model, texture and sky below is from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) under **CC0** (public domain). For the web the
textures were resized and converted to WebP, the skies to JPEG, and the models' glTF files compressed (meshopt geometry, WebP
textures); nothing else was changed. The hamsters, food, hats, effects, sounds and music are made in code for this game.
